# Workboard — shared design

Reference: user's own ERP prototype Variant C, read-only. Preserve Kanit, warm paper/surface, terracotta accent, 200px sidebar, 52px header and toolbar. Reports use a 180px project index with document content beside it, fine separators, restrained 12–15px text. Use the same shell for overview, Kanban, weekly report, calendar and access management. No hero or oversized dashboard tiles.

Calendar: follows the WorkAlljob/Lark concept in Workboard colours — 232px side panel (mini month, selected-day agenda, filters for meetings / pending tasks / released tasks and per-project colour toggles, remembered per browser) beside a month grid of six Monday-first weeks or a seven-column week view. Meetings are filled project-colour dots on a light tint, pending tasks are rings on their planned go-live date, released tasks show a check and strike-through on their actual Bangkok release date, overdue pending tasks are red. Cells show as many rows as fit, then "+N เพิ่มเติม" expands that week. Below 900px the side panel becomes a sheet and the agenda moves under the grid; below 640px month cells show dots only and the week view stacks days. Meetings have dates only (no times) by user decision, so there is no hourly grid. Meetings open in a right drawer with separate readable and editable tabs. Imported summaries keep their wording.

Visual review: checked in Playwright on 2026-09-18 at 320/375/414/768/1440px for overview, board, report, calendar, access and both drawers; no page-level horizontal overflow. Toolbars wrap to a second row below 540px instead of shrinking controls; the Kanban board keeps its intentional horizontal column scroll.
